# Portada, guía y juego (index, juego, places)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor decides where to eat in San Francisco and goes. Audience: people in SF on a phone, Christian's friends; Christian himself reviews this demo.
Job: find a place Chris recommends near you, by filters or by ten tap-only questions that end in a plan.
Proof: Chris's own photos, notes and reviews; distances from the visitor's location; his reels and Instagram posts where they exist.
Constraints: brand name CookFoodie exactly as the original site, the original chef logo as the only face (no photos of Christian), bilingual EN/ES, reviews stay in English, no invented photos or ratings, works without location.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A food guide where Chris's own food photos and words lead. It refuses themed costumes (the earlier "camioneta" concept was rejected by the user on 13-oct-2026: too many colors, unrelated to food, exaggerated type, used his face).

OWN-WORLD: Neutral warm-gray ground in light, near-black in dark, white cards. One accent: the CookFoodie orange (#e2550b light, #ff7a33 dark). One family, Geist, with weight and tight tracking for hierarchy. Pill buttons and chips, 16px cards. The chef logo in the header, the game host and the plan band.

STORY: The visitor sees real dishes, reads "Where Chris eats in San Francisco", taps Near me and watches the list re-sort with distances, or plays Cvander's ten questions and gets a titled plan (meal, cuisine, dish, company, drink) with a lead place and two backups.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left, headline, one sentence, Near me (orange) and Plan my meal (outline), and a live line naming the closest place. Right, a four-tile mosaic of Chris's photos that rotates one tile at a time and links to each place. On phones the mosaic goes first, two tiles, then the copy and actions.

SIGNATURE: The guide re-sorts with a view transition when location or filters change. The game advances on a ten-segment progress bar, Cvander answers each tap with a line in Chris's voice, and the result titles the plan from the answers.

FORM: Category standard executed with care (the canon path), chosen by the user after rejecting the rolled direction; original seed key 74f69d78.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
